GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the things people ask us most before they move.

How early should I book my move?

We recommend booking at least 5–7 days ahead, especially for weekends and month-end. Short-notice moves are possible depending on availability.

Are my belongings safe during the move?

Yes. Our trained crew uses quality packing materials and secures every item carefully while loading, transporting and unloading.

How is the moving cost calculated?

The quote depends on distance, volume of goods, floors and any extra services such as dismantling or storage. You get a clear estimate before the move with no hidden charges.

Do you provide packing materials?

Yes, we bring boxes, bubble wrap, tape and protective covers, and our packers can pack everything for you.

Can you store my belongings if my new home isn't ready?

Absolutely. We offer secure short and long term storage so you can move in whenever you are ready.
