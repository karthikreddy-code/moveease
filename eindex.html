Good to know

Questions we hear often

How do I book a move?

Send the quote form below or call us. We confirm your date, plan the move and share a clear price before anything is packed.

How early should I book?

A week ahead gives you the most choice of dates, but we also handle short-notice and weekend moves when our crews are free.

Do you move offices and vehicles too?

Yes. We handle office relocations and car or bike transport along with home shifting.

What if something gets damaged?

Tell your move manager at delivery. We will review it with you and work out the next step.
