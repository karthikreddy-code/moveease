What you get

Packing done by people who pack every day

Packing is the part of moving that takes the most time and causes the most damage when it is rushed. Our packers know how to wrap a glass, fold a wardrobe and box a television, so you do not have to learn on moving day.

You can book packing together with a full move, or only packing if you want to handle the transport yourself. Either way, every box is sealed, labelled by room and listed on your inventory checklist.

Packing for every item

The right method for each kind of item

Different belongings need different care. Here is how our packers handle the most common ones.

Crockery and glass

Each piece is wrapped separately and packed upright with padding in the gaps.

Electronics

TVs, computers and appliances are cushioned and protected from knocks and dust.

Clothes and wardrobes

Hanging clothes go into wardrobe boxes. Folded clothes are packed in sealed cartons.

Frames and mirrors

Glass and frames are wrapped, edge-protected and packed on their sides.

Good to know

Questions about packing

Can't find your answer? Call or message us and we will help right away.

Can I book only packing?

Yes. Packing can be booked on its own or combined with loading, transport and unpacking.

Which materials do you use?

Sturdy cartons, bubble wrap, foam sheets, packing tape, corner guards and furniture covers. Wardrobe boxes are available for hanging clothes.

Should I pack anything myself?

You may want to pack personal items, important documents and valuables yourself and keep them with you. Our packers handle everything else.

How long does packing take?

A 2 BHK home is usually packed in a day with a crew. We confirm the time during the survey.

Do you remove the empty boxes after unpacking?

If you book unpacking, we can take the empty boxes away so your new home stays clear.
