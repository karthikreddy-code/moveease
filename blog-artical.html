The complete home shifting checklist: what to do 4 weeks before you move

Moving feels simple until the last week. This guide breaks your move into small weekly tasks, so nothing is left to the last minute.

Quick summary

  • Book your movers and fix your date at least four weeks ahead.
  • Declutter first, so you only pack and pay to move what you need.
  • Pack room by room, starting with the rooms you use least.
  • Keep an essentials bag and valuables with you on moving day.
  • Check your inventory list when everything is delivered.

1 Why a four-week plan works

Most moving stress comes from decisions made in a rush. A little planning each week turns one giant job into a series of small, easy ones.

When you start four weeks ahead, you have time to compare quotes, choose a date that suits you, clear out what you no longer need and pack at a relaxed pace. You also avoid the last-minute charges and mistakes that come with hurried moves.

This checklist follows a simple rhythm: plan in week four, declutter in week three, pack in week two, finish up in week one, and then enjoy a smooth moving day. Tick each task as you finish it. We save your progress on this device.

Short on time?

Moving in less than four weeks? Do the Week 4 tasks today, then combine Weeks 3 and 2. A professional packing service can also save several days.

4 weeks to go

2 Plan and book

This week is about decisions. Once your date and movers are fixed, everything else falls into place.

Ask your building first

Apartment societies often allow moves only at certain hours and may need the service lift booked in advance. Check at both your old and new address.

3 weeks to go

3 Declutter and gather supplies

Every item you do not move is one less box to pack, carry and pay for. Go room by room and sort things into four piles: keep, donate, sell and discard.

2 weeks to go

4 Start packing room by room

Begin with the rooms and items you use least, and finish with the ones you use every day. This order keeps your home liveable until the end.

01

Store room and guest room

Pack first. These items are rarely needed before moving day.

02

Books and decor

Use small boxes for books because they get heavy quickly. Wrap frames in bubble wrap.

03

Off-season clothes

Fold into large boxes or wardrobe boxes. Keep this season's clothes for last.

04

Kitchen

Wrap each plate and glass. Keep a small cooking kit out until the final day.

05

Bathrooms

Seal bottles in zip bags and pack toiletries you use daily in your essentials bag.

06

Living room

Pack last. Photograph cable setups before unplugging any electronics.

Pack smart

Put heavy items in small boxes and light items in large boxes. A box you can lift comfortably is a box that is less likely to break or hurt someone.

1 week to go

5 Final preparations

The final week is for finishing touches. Aim to have everything packed except the things you need each day.

Valuables travel with you

Keep jewellery, cash, laptops and important documents in a bag that stays with you, not on the truck.

Moving day

6 Make moving day run smoothly

If you have planned well, moving day is mostly about supervising. Here is how the day usually flows.

Early morning: final walkthroughCheck every room, cupboard and balcony. Note meter readings and take photos of the empty rooms.
Crew arrives: share your prioritiesPoint out fragile items, items to load last and boxes that must be handled with extra care.
Loading: check items as they goTick items off your inventory list while the crew loads. This is the easiest way to avoid missing items.
Before you leave: one last checkOpen every drawer and cupboard. Make sure windows and doors are locked, and lights and gas are off.
At the new home: direct by room labelShow the crew where each room is so boxes go to the right place the first time.

7 After you arrive: the first 48 hours

Do not try to unpack everything at once. Focus on making your new home comfortable and safe first.

  • Unpack the essentials first: bed, kitchen basics, bathroom items and your essentials bag.
  • Check utilities: electricity, water, gas and internet should all be working.
  • Inspect your belongings: if anything is damaged, tell your move manager right away with photos.
  • Say hello to neighbours: and learn the building rules for garbage, parking and deliveries.
  • Finish address updates: complete anything you missed, such as licences and subscriptions.

8 Mistakes to avoid

After thousands of moves, these are the mistakes our crews see most often.

Booking movers too lateGood dates fill up quickly, especially weekends and month-ends.
Not asking for an itemised quoteA single lump-sum price makes it hard to compare and easy to add extras.
Packing everything the night beforeRushed packing leads to broken items and missing things.
Skipping labelsUnlabelled boxes make unpacking slow and stressful.
Packing valuables with general goodsKeep important documents and valuables with you.
Forgetting society rulesMissing lift or move-in rules can delay your move for hours.

9 Common questions

How early should I book movers?

Ideally three to four weeks ahead, especially for weekends and month-ends. Short-notice moves are often possible too. Call us and we will check availability for your date.

Should I pack myself or hire packers?

If you have time, packing low-risk items such as books and clothes yourself can save money. For fragile items, kitchens and furniture, professional packers use the right materials and technique.

What should I not put in the moving truck?

Valuables, jewellery, cash, important documents, medicines, laptops, and anything flammable or perishable. Carry these with you.

How do I protect furniture during the move?

Wrap surfaces in blankets or foam, cover sofas and mattresses, and use corner guards. Professional crews do this as part of a standard move.
