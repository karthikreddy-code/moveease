What affects the price

Six things that decide your quote

Every move is different. These are the factors we look at when we prepare your fixed quote.

Distance

Moves within the same locality cost less than long-distance and intercity routes.

Volume of belongings

More furniture, appliances and boxes need a bigger vehicle and a larger crew.

Floors and lift access

Stairs, narrow lanes and lift availability at both addresses affect the time needed.

Packing level

Basic packing, premium materials or full packing and unpacking are priced.

Moving date

Weekends, month-ends and busy seasons may affect availability and price.

Special items

Pianos, artwork, large appliances or fragile pieces may need extra handling.
